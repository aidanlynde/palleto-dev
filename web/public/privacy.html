1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you provide to us. We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you register for an account, use the app, or contact us for support. This includes:

  • Name and email address (via Google or Apple sign-in)
  • Images you upload or capture within the app for scanning
  • Project context information you provide (descriptions, creative direction, references)
  • Support messages you submit

Information collected automatically. When you use Palleto, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data and feature interactions (via analytics)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • IP address and general location (country/region)

Payment information. If you purchase Palleto Pro, payment is processed through Apple's App Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information. Purchase status and entitlement verification is handled by RevenueCat.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Palleto app and its features
  • Analyze images you upload to extract color palettes and generate creative direction
  • Save your inspiration cards and project context to your account
  • Verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you
  • Monitor usage patterns to improve performance and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use third-party providers to operate the app, including Firebase (authentication and database), OpenAI (AI analysis of images), RevenueCat (subscription management), and analytics providers. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their services.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business transfers. If Palleto is involved in a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Do We Offer AI-Based Products?

Yes. Palleto's core feature uses AI to analyze images you capture or upload and generate creative direction, color palettes, and project briefs. Images you submit are processed by OpenAI's API for this purpose. We do not use your images to train AI models beyond what OpenAI's standard API terms permit.

AI-generated outputs are suggestions only. You should review any output before acting on it professionally.

5. How Do We Handle Social Logins?

Palleto offers sign-in with Google and Apple. When you use these options, we receive basic profile information (name and email) from the provider. We do not receive your password or full social profile. Your use of these sign-in methods is also governed by the respective provider's privacy policy.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We retain your account information and inspiration cards for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it longer.

Images uploaded for scanning may be retained in cloud storage temporarily for processing and are not used for any purpose beyond generating your inspiration card.

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted data transmission (TLS), Firebase's secure authentication, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

Palleto is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (where technically feasible)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at aidanlynde@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track preference. Because there is no uniform standard for responding to DNT signals, we do not currently respond to them. If a standard is adopted in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

11. Do United States Residents Have Specific Privacy Rights?

Depending on your state, you may have additional rights under state privacy law (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act). These rights may include the right to know what personal data is collected, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of certain data sharing. To submit a request, contact us at aidanlynde@gmail.com.

We do not sell personal information and have not done so in the past 12 months.

12. Do We Make Updates to This Notice?

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may notify you via the app or email. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
